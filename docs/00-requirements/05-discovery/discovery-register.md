# Discovery register

> Seeded 2026-10-05 from the discovery work. **This file is now the source of truth — edit it directly.**
> Every seeded row starts as `Proposed` (AI-drafted). Review each one and change it to `Accepted`, `Changed` or `Rejected`. The count of reviewed rows is evidence for brief §21.

Brief §22 asks for discovery of what the brief does **not** say. This register is the master list of gaps.
Conflicts, ambiguities and assumptions — three of the eleven §22 categories — have their own documents because they need more than one line each:

- [conflicts-and-ambiguities.md](conflicts-and-ambiguities.md)
- [assumptions.md](assumptions.md)

Three categories marked *(beyond §22)* are added deliberately. A checklist only finds what it names; the domain/identity, data/privacy and testability gaps contain some of the highest-impact findings and none of §22's eleven headings would have surfaced them.
